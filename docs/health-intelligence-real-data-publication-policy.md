# Real Data Publication Policy

No collected observation is public by default. Publication requires an approved and sufficiently trusted source, an exact MMS product resolution, verified comparison basis, human verification, real-verified data classification and explicit publication approval.

Demo, real-unverified and real-verified records remain distinct at storage level. Demo data is never current market pricing. Basis-unverified observations may support investigation but cannot enter headline percentage comparisons. Low or unknown trust requires a documented manual override and audit justification.

Registration, availability, access feasibility and clinical suitability are separate concepts. Publication never implies legal importability, availability to an individual patient, clinical suitability, a recommendation to switch, or achievable savings.

Collector, reviewer and publisher permissions are separable. Audit and evidence history is immutable through ordinary operational interfaces.
