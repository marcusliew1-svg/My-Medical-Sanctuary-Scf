# Assisted Extraction

Assisted extraction is suggestion-only. It can normalize whitespace, case, known unit spacing, and simple pack notation, and it can propose brand, ingredient, strength, form, pack, price, and currency fields from structured or semi-structured material.

Every suggestion retains the original source value, suggested value, and parsing confidence. Thai, Bahasa Malaysia, and English wording remains stored separately from internal English labels. Transliteration or translation must never replace source text.

The deterministic Release 2A matcher produces exact, likely, ambiguous, no-match, or safety-exception proposals and shows matched and mismatched dimensions. A human remains authoritative. Assisted extraction cannot approve a source, certify a product identity or generic relationship, verify a price, or approve publication.
