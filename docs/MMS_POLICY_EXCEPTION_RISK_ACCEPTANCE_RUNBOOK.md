# MMS Policy Exceptions, Waivers & Risk Acceptance Runbook

**Document ID:** MMS-GOV-RUN-EXC-001  
**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

## Scope

This runbook governs policy exceptions, control waivers, temporary deviations, emergency exceptions and formal risk-acceptance records.

T6.34 does not itself approve an exception, accept a risk, waive a control, override Production, or authorize a prohibited clinical/regulatory/privacy/security action.

## Core principle

An exception is a governed, time-limited departure from an existing requirement. It is not a hidden rewrite of that requirement.

Every approved exception must be:
- specific;
- attributable;
- evidenced;
- time-bound;
- reviewable;
- revocable.

## Approval evidence

APPROVED and APPROVED_WITH_CONDITIONS require:
- approval reference;
- approver role;
- effective timestamp;
- expiry timestamp;
- evidence reference.

HIGH and CRITICAL exceptions additionally require documented compensating controls.

## Expiry

An exception expires automatically in governance terms when its expiry time passes. An expired exception must not be treated as continued approval merely because nobody closed the record.

Renewal requires a fresh review and evidence. Reusing an old approval reference without reassessment is not acceptable.

## Emergency exceptions

Emergency conditions may justify compressed review timing but do not eliminate:
- named ownership;
- evidence;
- risk rating;
- compensating controls;
- expiry;
- retrospective review.

## Non-waivable boundaries

Creating an exception record does not by itself override:
- applicable law or regulation;
- professional licensure;
- clinical privilege;
- Medical Director authority;
- privacy/data-protection obligations;
- security requirements imposed by external authority;
- insurer or contractual requirements.

## Closure

Closure/revocation evidence should document the final state, including whether the underlying control/policy was restored, replaced, formally changed, or the activity ceased.

## Production boundary

T6.34 does not create Production overrides, enable bypass flags, weaken existing security/clinical gates, or activate a clinical capability.
