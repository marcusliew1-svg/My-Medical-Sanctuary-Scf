Release 1B is visual and editorial architecture only. The following areas require formal medical, legal and regulatory review before stronger claims, indexing changes or campaign traffic.

- MSC / stem-cell pathway pages and any reference to regenerative outcomes.
- Exosome-related services.

- CAR-T and oncology-related education.
- MCED / multi-cancer early detection pages.

- Medicine Intelligence and international medicine access copy.
- Malaysia / Thailand regional care and care-travel claims.

## Review Questions

- Is the service currently offered, planned, referred, or educational only?
- What entity, clinician or licensed provider is responsible?
- What claims are allowed under Malaysian healthcare advertising rules?
- What claims require jurisdiction-specific review for Thailand, Singapore, Indonesia, Australia, Gulf markets or the US?
- Should the page be indexable, noindexed, gated or rewritten?
- What disclaimer is required for the specific topic?

## Copy Principles Until Approved

- Use educational framing.
- Use suitability and professional-review language.
- Avoid disease-treatment promises.
- Avoid public dosage, price, prescribing, dispensing or availability claims.
- Do not imply operational facility capability before approval.
