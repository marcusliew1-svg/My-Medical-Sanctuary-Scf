# MMS Privacy/Security Incident Assessment & Notification-Decision Runbook

**Document ID:** MMS-PRV-RUN-PSA-001  
**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

## Scope

This runbook governs assessment of potential privacy/security incidents, risk to individuals, notification applicability and notification evidence.

T6.42 does not declare that any real incident is a personal-data breach, does not determine a statutory deadline, and does not complete any authority or data-subject notification.

## Incident linkage

Every assessment must link to an existing MMS incident. The assessment does not replace the incident lifecycle.

## Assessment dimensions

Record, where known:
- jurisdiction;
- assessment type;
- affected data classes;
- approximate affected-subject count;
- exposure scope;
- risk to individuals;
- notification applicability;
- authority-notification state;
- data-subject-notification state;
- decision and supporting evidence.

## Notification decision

A notification decision other than NOT_ASSESSED requires:
- legal/privacy review reference;
- decision reference;
- immutable evidence.

Incident severity alone is not enough to determine notification obligations.

## Notification completion

Authority notification and data-subject notification are tracked separately.

A state of COMPLETED requires a stable notification reference. T6.42 does not create any statutory deadline; timing must come from authoritative jurisdiction-specific sources and, where applicable, the T6.32 regulatory-obligations register.

## Append-only history

Privacy/security assessments are immutable. If facts or legal analysis change, create a new assessment so earlier conclusions remain visible.

## Production boundary

No regulator portal integration, automated notification, public breach notice, patient notification, or Production workflow is enabled by this phase.
