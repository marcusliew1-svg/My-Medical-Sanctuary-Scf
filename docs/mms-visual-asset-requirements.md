# MMS Visual Asset Requirements - Release 1B

Release 1B uses existing repository assets only. Final launch quality requires a curated MMS visual system with real or approved generated imagery.

## Homepage Hero

- Physician-led consultation scene with affluent 45-65 patient energy.
- Warm private-clinic interior, not hospital ward or spa stock.
- Patient and professional interaction should feel calm, discreet and credible.

## Bangsar

- Hospitality-led wellness and longevity flagship visuals.
- Reception, private consultation, quiet waiting lounge and premium service detail shots.
- Use only once operational or approved as planned/opening-soon visual language.

## SS2

- More clinical and specialist-care tone.
- Renal/dialysis context requires approved facility photography before public claims.
- Avoid implying operational services until status and compliance are confirmed.

## Johor

- Advanced medical / ACC / laboratory hub visual direction.
- Needs approved roadmap imagery that distinguishes planned capability from active operations.
- Avoid manufacturing, lab or treatment visuals unless capability is verified.

## Doctors

- Approved doctor portraits.
- Approved names, credentials, registrations and specialties.
- Medical director statement image.
- Team interaction photography with patients.

## Mature Healthy-Ageing Patients

- Asian mature adults, couples, executives and family-oriented patients.
- Natural, dignified, warm, medically credible lifestyle.
- Avoid influencer, luxury-fashion or generic wellness poses.

## Diagnostics

- Screening, results review, body composition, imaging and consultation context.
- Human interpretation should be visible; avoid cold machine-only imagery.

## Health Intelligence

- Editorial visuals for global healthcare, medicine access, data review and patient education.
- Should feel like premium medical intelligence, not discount pharmacy or finance dashboard.

## My Sanctuary

- Tasteful future patient relationship layer.
- Appointment, continuity, health roadmap, privacy and Ling-supported guidance.
- No fake EMR screens with sensitive data.

## Ling

- Fewer appearances across the public site.
- Use Ling only where she improves orientation: education, question preparation, concierge guidance and future My Sanctuary.
- Balance Ling with physician, patient, diagnostics, interiors and location imagery.
