# Malaysia, Thailand and Singapore Rollout

Release 2C.1 operationalizes Malaysia, Thailand and Singapore while retaining all seven markets in the data model.

Malaysia begins with controlled onboarding of government or regulatory references, licensed pharmacies, hospital references and manufacturer or distributor information. Thailand follows the same workflow while preserving original Thai text in UTF-8 and keeping normalized internal identity separate. Singapore supports pharmacy retail, hospital, manufacturer and government reference observations without assuming their pricing bases are equivalent.

The rollout measures accuracy, reviewer throughput, stale-data handling and publication discipline before adding market volume. Australia, the United States, the UAE and Indonesia remain architecturally supported but are not operational targets for this release.
