# MMS Complaints, Concerns & Speak-Up Governance Runbook

**Document ID:** MMS-GOV-RUN-CCS-001  
**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

## Scope

This runbook governs complaints, operational concerns, privacy/security concerns, clinical-safety concerns and good-faith speak-up reports.

T6.31 does not itself create a hotline, appoint investigators, receive real complaints, notify regulators, or activate Production case intake.

## Lifecycle

Cases use:

`OPEN -> TRIAGED -> INVESTIGATING / ACTION_REQUIRED -> RESPONSE_PREPARED -> RESOLVED -> CLOSED`

A RESOLVED case requires a resolution summary, evidence reference and resolution timestamp. A CLOSED case additionally requires closure timestamp and completed external/regulatory reporting assessment.

## Triage

Complaints must be classified by type and severity. A customer-service label must not suppress a safety, privacy, security, fraud, misconduct or regulatory issue.

Clinical-safety concerns require immediate linkage into the approved clinical-safety/incident process.

Privacy/security concerns require the relevant privacy/security assessment.

Material operational findings may require CAPA or risk treatment.

## Speak-up and non-retaliation

Good-faith reporting must not result in retaliation because the concern was raised.

Where conflicts exist, the subject of a concern must not be the sole investigator or closure approver.

Anonymous/confidential-source handling must preserve confidentiality and avoid unnecessary identification.

T6.31 does not claim that an anonymous reporting channel or whistleblowing hotline is currently configured.

## Evidence and closure

Before closure:
1. preserve relevant evidence;
2. record scope and classification;
3. manage conflicts;
4. record incident/CAPA/privacy/clinical/regulatory linkages;
5. document resolution and evidence;
6. assess external/regulatory reporting need;
7. record complainant-notification state where applicable and safe.

## Existing governance integration

The existing CAPA model already recognizes `COMPLAINT` as a source type. T6.31 adds the missing complaint/concern system-of-record and links it conceptually to incidents, CAPA, risks and privacy/clinical governance without fabricating case data.

## Production boundary

No Production intake form, hotline, email routing, case-management integration, regulator notification workflow, or clinical-service capability is activated by this phase.
