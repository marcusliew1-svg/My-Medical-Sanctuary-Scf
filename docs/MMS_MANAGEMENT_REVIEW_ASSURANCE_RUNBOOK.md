# MMS Management Review, Governance Reporting & Executive Assurance Runbook

**Document ID:** MMS-GOV-RUN-MR-001  
**Status:** WORKING DRAFT

## Scope

This runbook governs periodic management review of the MMS operating system.

T6.33 does not itself conduct a management review, appoint a chair, approve Production readiness, certify compliance, or make a legal/clinical/regulatory conclusion.

## Review lifecycle

`PLANNED -> IN_PREPARATION -> IN_REVIEW -> ACTIONS_OPEN / COMPLETE`

A review may be cancelled only before completion.

## Minimum evidence pack

Management review should include, where applicable:
- enterprise risks and treatment state;
- control effectiveness and overdue tests;
- incidents and post-incident actions;
- CAPA and overdue remediation;
- complaints/concerns and speak-up trends;
- audit findings;
- regulatory obligations and licensing blockers;
- third-party/vendor assurance issues;
- workforce training and competency gaps;
- privileged-access review findings;
- change/release readiness;
- backup/recovery/continuity evidence;
- launch-capability blockers.

## Assurance conclusion

The permitted conclusion values are:
- `ADEQUATE`;
- `ADEQUATE_WITH_CONDITIONS`;
- `INADEQUATE`;
- `NOT_ASSESSED`.

A management assurance conclusion applies only to the reviewed scope and evidence period. It must not be presented as a regulatory approval, legal opinion, clinical approval or external certification.

## Completion evidence

A COMPLETE review requires:
1. evidence-pack reference;
2. minutes reference;
3. action-register reference;
4. non-NOT_ASSESSED assurance conclusion;
5. completion timestamp;
6. approval reference.

## Open issues remain open

Closing a management review must not automatically close:
- incidents;
- CAPA;
- complaints;
- risks;
- audit findings;
- regulatory obligations;
- launch blockers.

Those records retain their own lifecycle and evidence requirements.

## Production boundary

A green or adequate management review does not itself authorize Production. Production still requires the separate release, launch, privacy, legal, clinical, licensing, insurance and other applicable approvals.
