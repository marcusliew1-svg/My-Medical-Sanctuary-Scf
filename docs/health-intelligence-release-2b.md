# MMS Health Intelligence Release 2B

Release 2B adds anonymous public discovery for medicine identity, market reference observations, generic relationships and medication cost framing. Public components consume the sanitized read model at `/api/health-intelligence/public`; they do not query normalized source, price or reviewer tables directly.

Only records that pass the Release 2A publication rules are eligible for live public output. Preview may show clearly labelled fictional demonstration records so the workflow can be reviewed. Demonstration records are never treated as current market pricing and can never be published.

The public journey is: search, confirm exact identity, compare compatible observations, understand access limitations, and request professional review. No purchase, import, prescription, switching or savings instruction is provided. Patient-specific review and saved data remain future My Sanctuary features.

Release 2C should begin with a controlled, high-quality data operation for Malaysia, Thailand and Singapore, while retaining all seven market schemas and validation rules from day one. Expansion to Australia, the United States, the UAE and Indonesia should follow once the first three-market workflow is accurate and operationally proven.
